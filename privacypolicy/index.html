Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you invite Ocean One to your Discord server or use our platform, we collect only essential data required for bot functionality:

  • Discord Guild (Server) IDs and channel configurations.
  • Basic command usage data for administrative and logging functions.
  • Information provided voluntarily through support tickets or verification modules.

2. How We Use Information

The data we collect is used strictly to operate, maintain, and provide the features of Ocean One within your Discord server (such as moderation logs, raid prevention, and custom settings).

3. Data Protection & Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your server's data. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to outside parties.

4. Data Deletion

If you remove Ocean One from your Discord server, your server's automatic configuration data can be purged from our systems upon request through our support server.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our official support channels.
